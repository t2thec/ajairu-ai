We do things differently. Our team believes in transparent pricing because we work primarily with SMEs, and SMEs need to know what things cost before they commit. So here is a straight-talking guide to what AI consulting should cost in the UK, what you get for your money, and how to avoid overpaying.

## The Problem with AI Consulting Pricing

The AI consulting market is still maturing, which means pricing is all over the place. We see three common problems:

**Price opacity**: Many firms refuse to share pricing until they have you on a call. This wastes your time and makes comparison shopping impossible.

**Enterprise pricing for SME needs**: Some firms charge enterprise rates for work that an SME does not need. You end up paying for a 100-page strategy document when you need a practical action plan.

**Overpromising and underdelivering**: Low prices can be just as dangerous as high ones. If a consultant promises a full AI transformation for £2,000, you are not getting a deal. You are getting a sales pitch.

The key to finding affordable AI consulting in the UK is understanding what each stage should cost and what you should receive for that price.

## What AI Consulting Should Cost in the UK

Here is a breakdown of typical AI consulting services and their price ranges for UK SMEs. These are based on our team's experience and market rates as of 2026.

### AI Opportunity Audit: £750 to £2,500

This is the entry point. An opportunity audit assesses your business to identify where AI can deliver the most value. It should include:

- A review of your current processes and data
- Interviews with key team members
- A prioritised list of AI opportunities
- A clear recommendation on whether AI makes sense for you right now

At the lower end (£750), expect a focused assessment of one or two business areas. At the higher end (£2,500), you should get a comprehensive review across your entire operation.

If a firm quotes significantly less than this, question the depth. If they quote significantly more for an audit alone, you are likely paying for brand prestige rather than substance.

### AI Strategy and Roadmap: £5,000 to £15,000

A strategy engagement takes the audit findings and turns them into a plan. This should deliver:

- A documented AI strategy aligned with your business goals
- A phased roadmap with specific projects, timelines, and budgets
- An ROI projection for each recommended initiative
- A risk assessment and mitigation plan

The price varies based on the size of your business and the complexity of your operations. A 15-person company with straightforward processes will be at the lower end. A 150-person company with multiple systems and locations will be at the higher end.

### AI Implementation: £8,000 to £30,000+

Implementation is where the strategy becomes reality. This covers building and deploying actual AI solutions. The range is wide because implementations vary enormously:

- A simple workflow automation might cost £8,000 to £12,000
- A custom AI integration with your existing systems might run £15,000 to £25,000
- A complex, multi-system implementation could be £30,000 or more

The key question is not "how much does it cost?" but "how much does it save?" A £20,000 implementation that saves £40,000 per year in labour costs pays for itself in six months.

### Fractional CAIO: £2,000 to £5,000 per month

A fractional Chief AI Officer provides ongoing strategic guidance without the cost of a full-time hire. This is ideal for SMEs that need regular direction but are not ready to commit to a dedicated AI leader.

At £2,000 per month, expect a few hours of strategic guidance and oversight. At £5,000 per month, you should get hands-on direction, team mentoring, and active project management.

Learn more about this option in our guide to the [fractional AI officer](/blog/why-your-business-needs-fractional-ai-officer) role.

### AI Training Workshops: from £1,500

Training is often the highest-ROI investment you can make. Even the best AI tools are useless if your team does not know how to use them.

A half-day workshop for up to 15 people typically starts at £1,500. Full-day or multi-session training programmes scale up from there. The content should be tailored to your business and your team's current skill level.

## How to Judge Whether You Are Getting Value

Price alone does not tell you whether you are getting a good deal. Here are the questions to ask any AI consultant before you commit:

1. **What exactly will I receive?** A clear deliverable list, not vague promises.
2. **What is the expected ROI?** They should be able to estimate the financial impact, even if it is a range.
3. **What happens if it does not work?** A reputable consultant will have a plan for when things go sideways.
4. **Who will actually do the work?** Make sure the people pitching are not different from the people delivering.
5. **What is not included?** Understanding the boundaries of the engagement prevents surprise costs later.

## Red Flags to Watch For

- **Guaranteed ROI claims**: No one can guarantee specific returns
- **Pushing one specific tool**: A good consultant recommends the right tool, not the one with the biggest commission
- **No mention of training**: Implementation without training means low adoption
- **Refusal to share pricing**: If they will not tell you what it costs, you cannot make an informed decision
- **No SME experience**: Enterprise consultants often struggle with SME constraints

## Why Affordable Does Not Mean Cheap

There is an important distinction between affordable and cheap. Affordable means fairly priced for the value delivered, within reach of an SME budget. Cheap means cutting corners, underdelivering, or leaving you with a solution that does not work.

Our team focuses on affordable AI consulting for UK SMEs by keeping overheads low, using proven frameworks rather than reinventing the wheel for each client, and being honest about what you need versus what you do not.

We would rather tell you that you do not need a £15,000 strategy and a £2,500 audit will do the job, than sell you something you cannot use. That is what affordable really means.

## The Cost of Doing Nothing

It is worth considering the cost of not investing in AI. While 76% of UK businesses cite high costs as a barrier, the cost of falling behind competitors who do adopt AI is often higher.

Businesses that implement AI effectively gain efficiency, reduce errors, and free up their teams for higher-value work. Businesses that wait may find themselves competing against more efficient rivals with lower costs and faster response times.

The question is not whether you can afford AI consulting. It is whether you can afford to let competitors pull ahead.
